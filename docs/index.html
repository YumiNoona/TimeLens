Privacy is the architecture

Your activity never needs to leave your computer.

TimeLens runs on your Windows PC and stores activity in a local SQLite database. There is no login, tracking pixel, remote dashboard, or hidden analytics service.

  • ✓Local-only storage
  • ✓No account or subscription
  • ✓No key contents—counts only
  • ✓Open source and inspectable
