# TimeLens 7.6.0

TimeLens 7.6 is a connection, onboarding, and activity-visualization polish release.

## Highlights

- Centers the first-install and update-complete experience and keeps the notice pending across restarts until it is acknowledged.
- Distinguishes browser extension states clearly: checking, connecting, connected, paired but offline, update required, and unavailable.
- Preserves the secure browser pairing across app, browser, and PC restarts, while keeping one-click revocation.
- Replaces the oversized activity heatmap with a compact GitHub-style contribution calendar, accessible daily tooltips, responsive scrolling, and activity-distribution intensity levels.
- Uses a full 12-month contribution history by default and improves pairing status details with browser version and last-seen information.

The desktop app, dashboard, installer, Chrome companion, and Firefox companion share version 7.6.0.
