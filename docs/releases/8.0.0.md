# TimeLens 8.0.0

TimeLens 8.0 is a dashboard-layout and visual-consistency release. The time-accounting, browser attribution, concurrent-media, and contribution-heatmap logic from 7.9 remain unchanged. The desktop application, dashboard, installer, Chrome companion, and Firefox companion all report version 8.0.0.

## Today

- Removes the redundant Focus breakdown card.
- Adds a compact Day snapshot with the current foreground state, most-used app, top category, concurrent web-media duration, and browser-visit count.
- Places Day snapshot directly beside Activity contributions with a deliberate 16-pixel layout gap and no unused internal column space.
- Keeps the existing contribution heatmap behavior, ranges, cells, labels, and interactions unchanged.

## History

- Moves At a glance to the right of the contribution heatmap.
- Keeps both cards in one centered, equal-height row on desktop.
- Preserves a single-column responsive layout on narrower windows.
- Does not modify heatmap rendering or stored activity data.

## Apps

- Replaces the stacked Time / share treatment with three columns: App, Activity share, and Time.
- Centers the share bar in its own column and keeps duration right-aligned for fast scanning.
- Simplifies the progress treatment by removing the outlined track and glow.
- Collapses cleanly on tablets and hides only the share visualization on very narrow screens.

- `TimeLens-Firefox-Extension.zip`
